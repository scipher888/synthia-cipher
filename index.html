About

Synthia Cipher is a pen name, used for professional privacy. The two publications are different promises to the reader: Signal & Noise is the rigorous side — adversarial review, claim-by-claim scrutiny, a final judgment before anything is published; Intuition is the early side — hunches and arguments still forming.

I use AI tools to draft and pressure-test the work. The editorial judgment, final wording, published claims, and any errors are mine. The portrait above is an illustration of the pen name, not a photograph of a real person.
