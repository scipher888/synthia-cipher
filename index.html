About

Synthia Cipher is a pen name, used for professional privacy. There used to be two publications, making different promises to the reader: Signal & Noise was the rigorous side; Intuition was the early side — hunches and arguments still forming. In August 2026 the two modes moved into one place. Every Signal & Noise piece now carries both: the essay shows my hand, the audit shows the work. Intuition closed, complete — its four issues stay as written.

I use AI tools to draft and pressure-test the work. The editorial judgment, final wording, published claims, and any errors are mine. The portrait above is an illustration of the pen name, not a real person.
